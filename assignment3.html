Alfredo Pasta

"

"Creamy,cheesy and delicious Alfredo pasta that is easy to make at home

Ingredient

  • 2 cups pasta
  • 250g chicken
  • 1/2 cup cheese
  • 1/2 cup cream
  • Salt and Black Paper

Instruction

  1. Boil the pasta in salted water.
  2. Cook the chicken with spices.
  3. Add the cream and cheese.
  4. Add the boiled pasta.
  5. Mix everything well and serve hot.
