History Of Hunza

Hunza has a long history as a former princely state in the northern mountains of Pakistan. It was ruled by the Mir of Hunza for many centuries and became part of Pakistan in 1947. The valley is known for its ancient forts, traditional villages, and rich cultural heritage.
